This skill synthesizes material from the MIT-licensed repositories
`op7418/Humanizer-zh` and `hardikpandya/stop-slop`, and from the public
Wikipedia essay "Signs of AI writing" maintained by WikiProject AI Cleanup.

Sources:

- https://github.com/op7418/Humanizer-zh (MIT)
- https://github.com/hardikpandya/stop-slop (MIT)
- https://github.com/blader/humanizer (upstream of Humanizer-zh)
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing

The material was reorganized and rewritten for this portable skills catalog
as an editing-only workflow (rewrite / review), with added honesty rules
against fabricated specificity, anti-overcorrection guidance, and
Chinese-native patterns not covered by the sources. English counterpart:
`humanize-en` in the same catalog.
